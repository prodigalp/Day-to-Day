Our Mission

A Directory You Can Actually Trust

FixIt Local was born out of frustration. Finding a reliable handyman, plumber, or electrician in Metro Manila shouldn't be a gamble. We built a platform that makes it easy to find vetted, reviewed professionals for any job.

We are not the service provider — we are your trusted resource. Every professional listed has been screened and verified before appearing on our platform.

  • Government-issued ID and license verification
  • Insurance and liability coverage checks
  • Minimum 2-year work experience requirement
  • Ongoing review monitoring and quality control
  • Instant deactivation for verified complaints
